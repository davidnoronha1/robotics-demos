Browser Robotics Demos

Interactive perception & planning demos, running entirely in your browser.

  • 1. Value iteration on a gridworld coming soon

    Dynamic programming solving an MDP — the value heatmap converging sweep by sweep, then the greedy policy crystallizing out of it.

  • 2. Camera model + distortion coming soon

    The pinhole model plus Brown–Conrady distortion, and the fact that undistortion is not simply the inverse formula.

  • 3. IMU attitude estimation

    Three attitude estimates from your own phone's sensors: gyro-only, accel-only, and complementary fusion. Pick up your phone and feel why fusion exists.

  • 4. Configuration space visualizer coming soon

    A 2-link planar arm in workspace, and its collision set rendered live as a bitmap in θ₁/θ₂ configuration space.

  • 5. Particle filter localization + SLAM coming soon

    Mapping, kidnapping, and relocalization — watch a particle cloud collapse into ambiguous clusters in a symmetric corridor.

  • 6. Webcam corner detection + optical flow coming soon

    Live FAST corners and Lucas–Kanade tracks on your camera feed. Point a phone at a desk, move it, watch features stick to real objects.
